# ⚡ Quick Start Guide - EGSP v2.0

## 🎯 Start in 30 Seconds

### Option 1: Quick Login (Fastest)
1. Open **`login.html`**
2. Enter: `player@egsp.com` + `password123`
3. Click "ĐĂNG NHẬP" ✓

### Option 2: Create Account
1. Open **`login.html`**
2. Click "Đăng ký"
3. Fill form (phone: `0912345678`, pass: `MyPass123`, confirm password)
4. Click "Tạo tài khoản"
5. Login with new account

---

## 🎮 What to Try

**After Login:**
- ✅ Click avatar (top right) to open profile menu
- ✅ Select a game card (Liên Quân, Free Fire, etc.)
- ✅ Choose denomination (10K - 1M VND)
- ✅ Select payment method
- ✅ Click "THANH TOÁN" to process
- ✅ View transaction history
